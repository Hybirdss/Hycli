# Provider identification images

These original provider marks identify the supported connections. They do not imply endorsement or partnership. Ownership remains with the respective providers.

- ChatGPT / OpenAI: original black and white blossom SVGs from the official [OpenAI logo archive](https://cdn.openai.com/brand/OpenAI-Logos-2025.zip), `OpenAI-logos (new)/SVGs/OpenAI-{black,white}-monoblossom.svg`.
- Claude: original 32px transparent orange mark linked by [claude.com](https://claude.com/), retrieved normally from [its CDN](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/4zrzovbb/claude-com/369b14e80ac643cc09dccd581ccb91f82b559190-32x32.png). See `docs/design/brand-research/claude-official-32.provenance.txt`.
- Anthropic: original corporate webclip from the official website CDN, retained as a source asset but the Claude product mark is used in the interface.
- xAI: original official documentation [light SVG](https://docs.x.ai/_next/static/media/favicon-light.1u6watcuoe8mg.svg) and [dark SVG](https://docs.x.ai/_next/static/media/favicon-dark.0-f2gt9doy0_1.svg).
- Z.ai: original [official logo SVG](https://z-cdn.chatglm.cn/z-ai/static/logo.svg), shared by the standard and Coding Plan connection cards.

Retrieved 2026-10-09. Display sizes and monochrome dark-theme presentation are applied with CSS; source vectors have not been redrawn.
